# Comprehension quiz (participant copy; read aloud for readers who prefer; one answer per question)

1. When the app says it **could not check** two medicines together, what does that mean?
   - a) The two medicines are safe to take together.
   - b) The app has no information about that pair, so nothing is known about it.
   - c) A doctor has approved the pair.
   - d) The two medicines are the same medicine.

2. What does the **confirm step** (checking your medicines) do?
   - a) It sends your list to your doctor.
   - b) It is where you tell the app that a medicine matches your paper; the interaction check and the daily schedule use what you confirm.
   - c) It changes the dose on your paper.
   - d) Nothing; it is only decoration.

3. What happens to your **photo** by default?
   - a) It is uploaded to a public website.
   - b) It is read on this computer and then discarded, unless you choose to keep it for the session.
   - c) It is kept for ever.
   - d) It is shared with your doctor.

4. The app lists an **interaction** between two of your medicines. What should you do?
   - a) Stop taking one of the medicines yourself.
   - b) Talk to your doctor or pharmacist about it.
   - c) Ignore it, because the app is only a prototype.
   - d) Take double the dose of the other medicine.

5. What does a **"draft translation" label** on a Tamil or Hindi text mean?
   - a) A native speaker has checked it.
   - b) It was written by a machine and has not yet been checked by a native speaker.
   - c) It is an official government translation.
   - d) It is always wrong.

6. Where can you **delete your data**?
   - a) You must send an email.
   - b) With the "Delete my data" button (icon or menu in the header, footer, or the Documents page).
   - c) Your data cannot be deleted.
   - d) By uninstalling the app.
