# Tasks (read each card aloud exactly as written)

Documents: `data/usability_samples/U1.jpg` (a clean sample with one medicine that needs a check), `U2.jpg` (a scan whose medicine name the app may misread), `U3.jpg` (a prescription whose medicines are known to interact). The mapping to the source documents is private. Open the app at the start page in the participant's language. Start the timer after reading each card.

---
### Card 1: Upload and review
*"Here is a sample prescription from a made-up patient. Please use the app to upload it and look at the medicines the app found. Tell me what you see and what you would do. Say 'done' when you have finished checking the list."*

Document U1. Success: the participant gives consent, uploads the file, reaches the check screen, reads the list, and confirms or notes the medicine that needs a check. Record: completed (the list was reviewed and saved), errors, time, help.

### Card 2: Fix a medicine that was read wrongly
*"This is another sample paper. One of the medicine names on the screen may not match the paper. Please make the screen match your paper and save your checks."*

Document U2. Success: the participant opens the picker for the flagged row, searches, chooses the drug that is printed on the paper (correct) and saves. Record: picker choice correct (yes/no), errors (for example saving without resolving, choosing another drug), time, help.

### Card 3: Read the results
*"Please open the results. Which two medicines does the app say may interact, and how serious is it listed? And what would you do next?"*

Document U3 (uploaded and confirmed in the same way). Success: names the two medicines the app lists as an interaction, states the listed level in the participant's words (or says the level is not given), and says they would talk to their doctor or pharmacist (not "stop a medicine"). Record: answers (neutral notes), errors, time, help.

### Card 4: Delete your data
*"You have finished with this app and want nothing of yours to stay in it. Please do that."*

Success: finds "Delete my data" (header icon or menu, footer or Documents page), reads the dialog and confirms, and sees the confirmation. Record: where they looked first, number of clicks, completed, time, help.
