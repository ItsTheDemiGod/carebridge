# Debrief script (about 2 minutes)

Say, in the participant's language:
1. "Thank you. That was a test of the app, not of you."
2. "The documents were made up. The app is a prototype and it does not give medical advice. If you have a real question about a medicine, please ask your doctor or pharmacist."
3. "Two things the app tried to show: 'could not check' means the app does not know, it does not mean safe; and the check step is where you make the screen match your paper."
4. Ask: "Was there anything that surprised or confused you?" and "What would you change first?" Write one or two neutral lines, no names.
5. "We recorded no names, photos, audio or video. If you want your answers not to be used, tell me now."
6. Offer to answer questions about the project. Thank them again.

Afterwards: use "Delete my data" in the app, close the tab, and fill any missing cells of the sheets while it is fresh.
